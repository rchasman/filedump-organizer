## How to redeem

Everything you need to know to redeem your Woolworths eGift Card.

Where

- Online (participating stores)
- In store What To redeem your Woolworths eGift Card you will need: In-Store: last 13 digits of the card number Redeem Online: full 19 digits of the card number For Balances: full 19 digits of the card number These can be found in your gift email, or, if you have one, in your Prezzee Wallet. How Redeem in store: Shop and scan your groceries as usual When it’s time to pay, select ‘Gift Card’ as a payment method. If you are using the Self Checkout, this will be under 'More payment methods'. If you have a barcode: Select 'Scan Barcode' and scan the barcode at the register (not on the Pin Pad). Then, enter the 4 digit PIN on the EFTPOS Pin Pad. If you don’t have a barcode: Follow the instructions on the EFTPOS Pin Pad: enter the 13 digit Gift Card number (also called ‘In store code’) in the Pin Pad, followed by the 4 digit PIN. Redeem online: Shop and scan your groceries as usual When it’s time to pay, select ‘Gift Card’ as a payment option Enter the 19 digit card number, followed by the the 4 digit PIN
